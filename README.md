## Publications

These are research projects I worked on during my final year of college (2023–2024), all published through IEEE.

**[Analysis of Cell Balancing Techniques for Li-ion Batteries](https://doi.org/10.1109/SSITCON62437.2024.10796529)**  
Shrinidhi Shambhoji, Raksha Kulal, Krithika Patgar, Parveen Khatal, Shweta Koraddi  
*SSITCON 2024 — IEEE* · 3 citations  
Comparative analysis of passive and active cell balancing topologies (switching shunt resistor, multiple inductor, flyback converter, single capacitor) simulated in MATLAB/Simulink to identify the optimal balancing strategy for EV battery management systems.

**[Thermal Management for EV Batteries Using Microcontroller](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/10763298)**  
Parveen Khatal, Krithika Patgar, Shrinidhi Shambhoji, Raksha Kulal, Shweta Koraddi  
*ICSES 2024 — IEEE* · 2 citations  
Temperature monitoring and threshold-based fan control system on an ARM Cortex-M3 microcontroller, with real-time LCD display — prevents thermal runaway and extends EV battery lifespan.

**[Analysis and Simulation of Cascaded H-bridge Multilevel Inverters](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/)**  
*ICONAT 2025 — IEEE*  
Simulation and performance analysis of cascaded H-bridge multilevel inverter topologies for power electronics applications.
